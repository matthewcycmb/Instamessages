# Konvo Reel Playbook

Research pulled live from LightReel (lightreel.ai) on 2026-08-19 across two angles: the doomscroll/detox audience and build-in-public founder content. Re-run anytime: `node --env-file=.env.local scripts/lightreel.mjs "question"`.

**Positioning line the whole account hangs on:** people want the *social* part of Instagram — DMs and friends — not the algorithmic *media* layer. Konvo's one-liner: **Instagram communication without Instagram consumption.**

**Content mix (LightReel's recommendation for this exact app):**
- 35% personal problem / real-user stories
- 25% objection & FAQ demonstrations
- 20% transparent experiments and numbers
- 10% failures, App Review issues, loophole fixes
- 10% direct product demos

**House rule (non-negotiable):** every number, failure, and receipt shown on camera is real. No invented downloads, MRR, or testimonials — if there's no real receipt, that reel waits.

## Angle A — Doomscroll/detox audience (problem-first)

### A1. Relatable confession → practical fixes
Open with an embarrassing scrolling moment, then demo 3–4 fixes on screen.
- Proven hooks: "A couple weeks ago I had a dream, and it was just me scrolling Instagram Reels." (instagram.com/reel/Da_CMkKxC2j) · "Easy ways to stop doomscrolling." (instagram.com/reel/DZ2_huRBQ4P)
- Why it works: confession makes it personal, not preachy; demos make it saveable.
- **Konvo version:** "I opened Instagram to reply to one message. That was two hours ago." → the trap is that your DMs live *inside* the feed → 3 fixes, last one: "so I deleted the app and kept the messages" + screen recording of Konvo DMs working.

### A2. Replacement list while doing the activity on camera
Numbered list of scroll-replacements while journaling/cooking/walking on camera.
- Proven hooks: "You fail to fix your relationship with your phone because you haven't replaced scrolling with anything." (instagram.com/reel/DM9FszTPjsb) · "10 things to do instead of doomscrolling before your brain rots." (instagram.com/reel/DZ_jOk8BIwN)
- Why it works: fills the empty habit loop most detox content ignores.
- **Konvo version:** "5 things I replaced the feed with — and the one part of Instagram I kept." Items 1–4 offline; item 5: my actual friends (DMs via Konvo, shown working).

### A3. "What it does to your brain" explainer
Talking head + one simple mechanism + captions/diagram.
- Proven hooks: "Does doomscrolling actually rot your brain?" (instagram.com/reel/DVOtJpoGcl5) · "How long have you been scrolling for? Did you lose track of time?" (instagram.com/reel/DWyjdhrCXki)
- Why it works: provocative question → self-recognition → mechanism satisfies curiosity.
- **Konvo version:** explain *the DM trap* mechanically: you open the app with intent (a message), and the feed is one thumb-slip away — variable reward does the rest. "The fix isn't willpower, it's removing the slot machine from the mailbox."

### A4. Relatable skit → phone-setting tutorial
Dramatize the failure, hard-cut to the setting that solves it.
- Proven hooks: "I've got a lot of work to do — let me just scroll for…" (instagram.com/reel/DSTW9VVDPvv) · "When you're about to fall asleep on your phone, so you put it away and randomly get that energy again." (instagram.com/reel/Da-iGs-COk9)
- Why it works: recognition + humor earns the attention the tutorial converts.
- **Konvo version:** skit: friend sends a Reel at 11pm → you reply → 40 minutes gone. Cut: "here's my phone now" — Instagram gone, Konvo notification comes in, reply, phone down.

### A5. Repetitive command / extreme personal rule
Blunt repeated interruption, or an unusually strict personal rule.
- Proven hooks: "When it feels like everyone is rich and living a luxury life… Delete Instagram." (instagram.com/reel/Dak4PENtHSq) · "Will you think I'm crazy if I tell you I delete Instagram three times a week?" (instagram.com/reel/DZsQTUiCqVC)
- Why it works: extreme-but-specific beats "use your phone less"; strong curiosity gap.
- **Konvo version:** "I haven't opened Instagram in 30 days. I also haven't missed a single message. Both of those are true." Then show how.

## Angle B — Build-in-public founder (talking head)

### B1. Relatable frustration → "so I built it" (LightReel: *best fit for Konvo*)
Personal frustration → why existing options failed → reveal at 5–12s → one unmistakable screen recording → soft CTA. 30–65s.
- Proven hooks: "Why can't we just remove Reels from Instagram?" — @getsociallite (instagram.com/reel/DamLOLxA0nS) · "I'm building Buds… and I just added widgets." — @jen__jpeg (instagram.com/reel/DamdCCjygXm)
- **Konvo version (the origin reel — make this first):** open Instagram to answer one message on camera → get pulled into Reels → "deleting Instagram was never an option, my group chats live there" → reveal: blocked feed and a working DM conversation *in the same shot*.

### B2. Transparent numbers + unresolved update
Result/number → dashboard receipt in first 5–10s → cause → what's still failing → next experiment. ~60s.
- Proven hooks: "TikTok gives you $6,000 of free ad credits" → 10K downloads, $460 MRR, broken attribution — @current.kev (instagram.com/reel/DbtTwMiONGJ)
- **Konvo version:** weekly real experiments from PostHog: activation rate, screen-time drop, pass usage. "Usage fell X%, but almost nobody disabled DMs" is a stronger story than a download count. Only numbers we can show on screen.

### B3. Founder failure → live fix
Concrete failure with stakes → show the broken state → fix on camera → lesson or open question. 38–60s.
- Proven hooks: "Is it possible to make a truly bug-free app?" — @chris.raroque, ~75K views (instagram.com/reel/Dbd6N1OKiVK)
- **Konvo version — one is live right now:** the App Review rejection of build 53 is a ready-made episode: show the rejection, explain the Family Controls entitlement mystery, document the resubmission. Also good: Instagram breaking a selector → the cage-patch hotfix shipping in 60s. Never manufacture failure; film it when it happens.

### B4. Objection/question → proof on the phone
Comment full-screen → one-sentence answer → demonstrate on a real device → external receipt (privacy label, settings) → invite the next question. 40–65s.
- Proven benchmark: @getsociallite's "Does it steal and sell my information?" — ~154K views (instagram.com/reel/DZ-CyLmh6EN). This format is working for the direct competitor.
- **Konvo versions, in priority order:** Can I still send/receive DMs? · Can I open a Reel someone sends without falling into the feed? · Do you see my Instagram login or messages? (answer with the privacy label on screen) · What stops me from bypassing the block? · Do notifications and group chats still work?

### B5. High-stakes serialized challenge
Numbered series with a real deadline/stake, scoreboard every episode. 30–50s.
- Proven hooks: "Is it possible to build an app, grow it, win $100K… in 61 days?" — @andov.zip (instagram.com/reel/DcEOyVmAkmX)
- **Konvo version:** stake reflects the mission, not a coding streak: "30 days to cut my Instagram time without missing a single important DM." Scoreboard: screen time, DMs answered, accidental opens.

### B6. Build/demo with a surprising constraint
Constraint → fast montage → working product in hand → one lesson. 25–50s.
- Proven hooks: "I built an app while running errands." (instagram.com/reel/DXZQN1cBb0Y)
- **Konvo version:** reserve for visible ships — closing a loophole a user found, shipping DM-only mode in response to one complaint. Skip for routine polish.

## The 8 rules that keep it from feeling like an ad

1. The founder has a problem before the product has a feature. "I kept losing an hour after checking one message" — never "seamlessly combines blocking with communication."
2. The app appears as *evidence*, not the opening pitch. (SocialLite's overt 25s product-pitch reel is the counter-example: instagram.com/reel/Da3IC0AO_g9.)
3. There is a visible receipt: Screen Time, working DMs, privacy label, the rejection email, the actual bug.
4. Include something unresolved — a perfectly resolved testimonial sounds scripted.
5. Admit trade-offs and wrong assumptions on camera.
6. Founder language, not company language: "I built", "I got this wrong" — never "we're excited to announce", "revolutionary", "seamlessly".
7. One reel, one claim. Prove "DMs still work" OR "shared Reels don't open the feed" OR "my screen time dropped" — never all in one post.
8. Soft, story-native CTA: "What loophole should I test next?" / "Follow — screen-time result next week." Not "link in bio" on repeat.
